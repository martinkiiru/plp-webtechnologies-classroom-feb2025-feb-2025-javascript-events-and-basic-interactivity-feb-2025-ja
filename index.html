Pet FAQ

How often should I feed my pet?

You should feed your digital pet at least twice a day to keep it happy and healthy.

Can my pet get sick?

Yes, if you neglect your pet for too long, it might become sad or unresponsive. Regular interaction is key!

How do I know if my pet is happy?

A happy pet will respond enthusiastically to your interactions and display a positive status message.
